Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

This website uses analytics to understand page performance and campaign results. Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, and Microsoft Clarity load only after you select “Allow both” in the “Analytics & marketing” prompt. Clarity provides session recordings and heatmaps to show how visitors use the site. This choice also permits advertising-related storage and personalization by Google, Meta, and Clarity. Vercel Analytics may provide privacy-focused site measurements separately.

Information collected

Analytics providers may process device, browser, referral, interaction, and campaign information. AI Income Lab does not collect payment details on this website. Membership checkout is completed on Skool and is governed by Skool’s policies.

Your choices

You may decline optional analytics and marketing tracking when prompted. You can reopen “Privacy choices” from the website footer, change your selection, or clear this site’s storage in your browser. Your analytics choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so the prompt does not reappear on every visit. The light or dark theme you pick with the header switch is saved there too.

YouTube videos

Video thumbnails load from YouTube’s image servers, which receive your IP address and browser details. Dedicated video watch pages load an embedded YouTube player when you open the page, before you press play, with autoplay off. Written guides that include a video use a click-to-load control, so their embed is created only after you press play. Without JavaScript those guide controls are links to YouTube. These players use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube receives connection information when its player loads. Following a “Watch on YouTube” link opens YouTube directly.

Email open tracking

Some emails from AI Income Lab and related projects, such as TubeAnalytics and VisiScan, include a small invisible image served from this website. When your email app loads that image, we record a code that identifies the email, the time it was opened, and your email app’s user agent, and store them in Airtable to see which emails were opened. Your IP address may appear in our hosting provider’s request logs but is not saved with that record. To prevent this, turn off automatic image loading in your email app.

Booking a call

The booking calendar loads from Cal.com when you scroll to the booking section with “Book a call” selected. The name, email, and notes you enter go to Cal.com to schedule the call and are governed by Cal.com’s privacy policy.

Sending a message

The contact form sends your name, email, and message to the site owner’s inbox through Resend, an email delivery service. They are used only to reply to you.

Weekly build notes

If you subscribe to the weekly build notes, your email address is stored in Resend’s contact list and used only to send those emails. Weekly broadcasts include an unsubscribe link. The welcome email lets you reply “unsubscribe” or use an email link to request removal. The confirmation email is transactional: ignoring it does not subscribe you. You can ask for your address to be deleted at any time.

Third-party services

This site may use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Microsoft Clarity, Vercel Analytics, YouTube, Airtable, Cal.com, Resend, and Skool. Review each provider’s privacy terms for details about their processing.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the community administrator at automojic@proton.me.
