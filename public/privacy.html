Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

This website uses analytics to understand page performance and campaign results. Google Analytics and Meta Pixel load only after you select “Allow analytics.” Vercel Analytics may provide privacy-focused site measurements separately.

Information collected

Analytics providers may process device, browser, referral, interaction, and campaign information. AI Income Lab does not collect payment details on this website. Membership checkout is completed on Skool and is governed by Skool’s policies.

Your choices

You may decline optional analytics when prompted. You can reopen “Privacy choices” from the website footer, change your selection, or clear this site’s storage in your browser. Your analytics choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so the prompt does not reappear on every visit.

YouTube videos

Video thumbnails on the home page load from YouTube’s image servers, which receive your IP address and browser details. The YouTube player loads only when you press play, and it uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com).

Email open tracking

Some emails from AI Income Lab and related projects, such as TubeAnalytics and VisiScan, include a small invisible image served from this website. When your email app loads that image, we record a code that identifies the email, the time it was opened, and your email app’s user agent, and store them in Airtable to see which emails were opened. Your IP address may appear in our hosting provider’s request logs but is not saved with that record. To prevent this, turn off automatic image loading in your email app.

Booking a call

The booking calendar loads from Cal.com only when you choose to book a call. The name, email, and notes you enter go to Cal.com to schedule the call and are governed by Cal.com’s privacy policy.

Third-party services

This site may use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Vercel Analytics, YouTube, Airtable, Cal.com, and Skool. Review each provider’s privacy terms for details about their processing.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the community administrator through AI Income Lab on Skool.
