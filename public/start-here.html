Choose your first AI automation project.

You don’t need to learn every AI tool at once. Pick the work you want to do, finish one small example, then add complexity. Every guide and video here is free without a Skool login; the tools themselves may need an account or charge for usage.

New to the idea? Read what AI automation is, with examples and a first build, before you choose a path.
