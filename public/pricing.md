# Pricing: AI Income Lab by Mike Holp

Reviewed November 12, 2026. AI Income Lab is the paid learning community on Skool (skool.com/ai-automation-station-7346). Prices are in USD and are charged by Skool. This site never sees payment details. Check the Skool plans page for current prices: https://www.skool.com/ai-automation-station-7346/plans

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | $29 | $290 | Self-directed learners building their first workflows and unlocking lessons as they participate |
| Premium | $49 | $490 | Builders ready to make automations, voice agents and avatar videos with immediate course access |
| VIP | $89 | $890 | Builders who want weekly group help with their projects and reusable n8n workflows |

Cancel before the next billing period from your Skool account.

## What each plan includes

- Standard: community, courses and tutorials; the Runable course; Beginner's Automation Course (unlocks at Skool level 2); VAPI AI Voice Agent Course (unlocks at level 4).
- Premium: everything in Standard, immediate access to the Beginner's Automation Course and the VAPI AI Voice Agent Course, and the Complete AI Avatar Video Course.
- VIP: everything in Premium, weekly live group coaching and Q&A, the Ultimate N8N Template Library (6,400+ workflows), and software deals.

## Limits and scope

- Coaching is weekly group coaching and Q&A for members implementing what they learn. Custom business implementation is separate and is not included in membership. Ask about project scope before joining.
- The Runable course is available to all members on every plan.
- No income is guaranteed.

## Free without joining

All written guides (https://www.ai-automation-station.com/guides.html) and YouTube videos are free and need no Skool login. The tools used in the guides may need their own accounts or charge for usage.
