Quick answer

My n8n runs on a Hostinger VPS for about 10 a month. The Shorts uploader polls a Google Drive folder hourly, downloads each new clip, uploads it to YouTube as unlisted and then writes a description, tags and title with AI before making it public, though its Apify transcript step failed in my demo. To use the Sora 2 node I first updated n8n to 1.118.1 with docker compose pull and a restart, then built a form that sends a prompt and product photo for an 8-second 1280 by 720 video. I also covered the 2.0 beta update and which routes exist for trying Sora 2.

This guide merges five videos from October to December 2025. They share one setup: a self-hosted n8n on a Hostinger VPS, which I bought with about 100 bundled templates. 1. A Shorts uploader from Google Drive (November 2025)

I cut Shorts with Opus Clip, download them and drop them in a Google Drive folder. A Google Drive trigger polls the YouTube Shorts folder every hour, and you authenticate Drive through Google Cloud Console. The workflow downloads the new file by ID and uploads it with the YouTube node, using the file name as the title and unlisted visibility. An HTTP request calls an Apify YouTube transcript actor, a GPT-4.1 nano node writes the description, a Gemini agent produces comma-separated tags and another step writes a title up to 100 characters. A final update sets the title, description with my community link and tags, notifies subscribers and sets the video to public. The upload worked, but the Apify transcript step failed in the demo, so the AI steps ran without a transcript.

2. The Sora 2 node (November 2025)

The Sora 2 option in the OpenAI node needs n8n 1.118.1 or later, so I updated my VPS first. In the Hostinger terminal, run docker compose pull, then docker compose down and bring it back up, and confirm the version in profile settings. I added an OpenAI video generation node with the Sora 2 model, set 8 seconds and 1280 by 720, and wired a form that takes a prompt and a product photo, resized before sending. A bad request error was a negative OpenAI balance, fixed with a 6 dollar top-up. The finished video did not look exactly like the product I uploaded. On n8n’s cloud, it should already be current.

3. Updating to n8n 2.0 beta (December 2025)

The announcement on December 8 stressed secure-by-default execution, reliability and performance, but I only skimmed those. After updating, I noticed a cleaner interface, easier movement between nodes from inside a node, a new execution spinner, a resizable sidebar and a separate publish step that splits saving from going live. I found the new features somewhat underwhelming. To update my VPS I pulled the 2.0 beta image and restarted with docker compose up -d. Back up your workflows before you update.

4. Six automations I use (November 2025)

I toured the self-hosted dashboard, with about 10 a month for the VPS and room to upgrade RAM and disk, and walked through a job application workflow, a LinkedIn newsletter automation, the Shorts uploader and an AI stock advisor, with templates in my community. For the job search build, see the n8n job search workflows, and for the LinkedIn one the YouTube to LinkedIn guide.

5. Trying Sora 2 without n8n (October 2025)

Right after launch I checked the routes. The official iOS app had weak reviews, as I recalled about 2.8 out of 5. Higgsfield needed a paid plan from about 9 a month, Sora2.run asked for a paid plan, Artlist asked for about 20 a month and HeyGen offered Sora 2 and Veo 3.1 features. In HeyGen, my one attempt at a Sora 2 UGC ad video, from a photo, was blocked by Sora’s content policy. I showed no finished video, so the title’s promise was not delivered. I quoted HeyGen Creator at 29 a month and Team at 39 with 4K, as of then.

FAQ

What n8n version does the Sora 2 node need?

1.118.1 or later. On a Hostinger VPS, update with docker compose pull and a restart.

Why did the Sora 2 call fail with a bad request?

My OpenAI balance was negative. A top-up fixed it.

Did the Shorts uploader work?

The upload and publish steps did. The Apify transcript step failed, so the AI copy ran without a transcript.
