Quick answer

In your Skool community open Settings, then Pricing, and switch on membership tiers: a free level plus one or two paid upgrades. In October 2025 I set mine to free, premium at $29 a month ($280 a year) and VIP at $89 a month ($850 a year), then locked classroom modules and live calls to each tier. Skool's Hobby plan was $9 a month with a 10% fee and Pro was $99 with 2.9%, as I noted then.

Who this is for and what you need

This guide is for an owner with a free Skool community who wants recurring paid upgrades without putting a paywall on the front door. I waited about four months for this feature because joins stopped whenever I made my community paid.

  • Good fit: a free community with an audience already in it. Mine had just over 1,000 members.
  • Not covered here: picking between Hobby and Pro. That is in the Hobby vs Pro review.
  • What you need: an existing Skool community, admin access to Settings and a plan for what each tier unlocks: training, templates, calls or coaching.

1. Pick the Skool plan

You create the community first. I compared the $9 a month Hobby plan, which had a 10% transaction fee, with the $99 a month Pro plan at 2.9% with all features. I was still on Hobby to keep costs down. Both start with a 14-day free trial.

2. Set up the tiers

Go to Settings, then Pricing, and choose the premium model: free to join, with one or two paid upgrade tiers. Mine were:

  • Free: community access and beginner training only, locked down on purpose so more people upgrade.
  • Premium, $29 a month or $280 a year: advanced training, ten done-for-you automation templates and weekly Q&A calls. The annual price is about 20% off.
  • VIP, $89 a month or $850 a year: everything, plus the AI coaching program and all classroom modules.

Existing paid members were moved to Premium automatically. Preview shows the plans page a new member sees when they click to join: they pick a plan first, then enter card details, and can choose monthly or annual for the paid levels.

3. Lock modules and calls by tier

In the classroom you can restrict each module to a tier. Tutorials needed Premium and the coaching program needed VIP. Before tiers I sold modules with a one-time price, such as $29 for one module. Now a monthly tier can unlock them instead, which also makes upgrading from free easier because members see what each level opens.

Setting up tiers, step by step

Here is the order I followed on the day the feature arrived, with the details that are easy to miss.

  1. Open Settings, then Pricing. Choose the premium model, which is free to join with one or two paid upgrade tiers.
  2. Keep the free tier thin. Mine included community access and beginner training only, so the upgrade has a clear reason.
  3. Price monthly and annual. Premium was $29 a month or $280 a year. VIP was $89 a month or $850 a year. Both annual prices worked out at about 20% off.
  4. Decide whether VIP stays on. The top tier can be switched off. I kept it for the AI coaching program.
  5. Check existing paid members. Mine were moved to Premium automatically.
  6. Click Preview. It opens the plans page a new member sees first. They choose a plan, pick monthly or annual on the paid levels and then enter card details.
  7. Gate the calendar and classroom. Live Q&A calls on the calendar were for Premium and VIP only. In the classroom, free members saw only the Get started and How-to guides modules. For any other module, set it to Private and choose the tier that unlocks it.
  8. Announce it. I posted in the community listing each tier and its benefits, with a link to the plans page and a screenshot.

Before tiers I sold single modules with a Buy it now price, such as $29 once. A monthly tier replaces that and makes the upgrade path easier to see. The classroom upsell tour shows the older one-time approach in more detail. If you want new free members to get an automatic email, pair this with the Skool welcome email automation. FAQ

Can a community be free and still earn?

Yes, with tiers: a free level plus paid upgrades. That was my reason for using them.

What did the Hobby and Pro plans cost?

In October 2025, $9 a month with a 10% transaction fee for Hobby and $99 a month with 2.9% for Pro, as I noted then.

Do existing paid members change?

In my community they were moved to the Premium tier automatically.

How do I lock a classroom module to a tier?

Set the module to Private, then pick the tier a member needs to open it. That replaced the one-time Buy it now price I used before.

Can members pay yearly?

Yes. Premium and VIP each had a monthly and an annual option, and the annual prices were about 20% lower.

How should I tell existing members about tiers?

I posted an announcement in the community that listed each tier and its benefits, with a link to the plans page and a screenshot.
