ChatGPT Image Editing: Sketch-to-Scene and Local Edits

Quick answer

Sketch a scene, generate the image, then make one local edit and look over the whole result. In my test a rough sketch of two windows, a chair and a pet came back in the right layout in about 25 seconds, and marking a spot and asking for a dog added one there. Check placement, reference fidelity and anything that changed without being asked.

I recorded this the day after OpenAI announced ChatGPT Images 2.5 on September 8, 2026. I already use ChatGPT images for my YouTube thumbnails, so I wanted to see the new Sketch feature and whether editing felt more precise. In the video I sketch a room, add a dog with a second sketch, try a few portrait templates on my own photo and work up a thumbnail.

This guide keeps the parts you can repeat: composing with a sketch, making one local edit, using personal references carefully, and the documented steps for doing it today. OpenAI’s help centre now describes ChatGPT Images 2.6, so expect some details to have moved since I recorded.

Begin with a composition you can compare

I clicked Sketch and drew two windows, something meant to be a chair that looked more like a briefcase, and a dog lying on the floor that looked nothing like a dog. My drawing skills don’t matter much here. What the sketch carries is placement and rough size.

ChatGPT filled in the prompt for me. It asked for original artwork inspired by the attached sketch, preserving its subject, emotion and gesture rather than its exact strokes or shapes. So the target is a composition, not a tracing, and that is how you should judge it.

For your first try, keep it to three or four objects and note beforehand which positions and proportions matter. Without that note, it’s easy to approve whatever comes back because it looks polished.

Inspect what the model changed

A progress indicator showed while it worked, and the image arrived in about 25 seconds. OpenAI’s announcement claimed up to 50% faster generation than Images 2.0. I only timed this one image, so I can’t confirm that figure.

The layout matched my sketch surprisingly well: a chair on the left, two windows with the left one smaller and the right one larger, as I drew them, and an animal on the floor on the right. In the video I called that animal a cat, even though I had drawn a dog. That is a good example of what to check: the arrangement can be right while a subject quietly changes. Go through each object in your note and look for anything missing, swapped or added.

Make one local image edit

Next I drew directly on the generated image and wrote “add a dog here”. The new version had a dog in the spot I marked. That was the cleanest edit in the whole video.

A narrow edit is easy to judge, so say what should change and, if it matters, what must stay the same. Then compare the full image with the previous version, not just the new object. OpenAI’s own help article warns that highlights are not always precise and edits may extend beyond the area you selected.

Try Sketch and Select yourself

When I recorded, I simply clicked a Sketch option on screen. The current Images in ChatGPT help article documents Sketch in the mobile app, and these are its steps:

  1. In the message box, type @ and select Sketch.
  2. Draw your sketch. You can pick a colour, erase parts or undo a stroke.
  3. Confirm the sketch with the checkmark.
  4. Add instructions describing the image you want, such as subject, colours or style.
  5. Send the message to generate the image.

For a local edit afterwards, the same article says to open the image, choose Select, highlight the area you want to change and describe the change. On mobile you choose Next after selecting, and a slider changes the size of the selection tool. You can also skip Select and describe the edit in the conversation, naming the area in your prompt. Undo, Redo and Cancel are there if the highlight goes wrong.

Use personal references deliberately

I tried the 80s flashback template with a photo of myself. Its prompt asked to preserve my identity, facial features, skin tone and appearance, and the result looked a lot like me. It is a style, though, not a real photo: I wasn’t even born in 1985.

I then used that generated image to ask for a caricature of me and my job based on everything ChatGPT knows about me. After 35 to 40 seconds it added my businesses in the background, including TubeAnalytics, my AI visibility startup and AI Income Lab, plus a passport, a camera, a microphone, a laptop and a sign about Thailand with words like automation, freedom and community. I wasn’t sure where some of that came from. The 3D designer toy template went further off brief and gave me things in my hair and palm-tree sunglasses I never asked for.

So use references you own or have permission to use, keep the original, and check identity, accessories, setting and any text the model invents.

Iterate on a thumbnail with plain feedback

I asked ChatGPT to pull a YouTube thumbnail from a URL and swap the person for me. It couldn’t fetch the thumbnail, so I uploaded the thumbnail and my reference photo instead. It replaced only the person on the right and left the one on the left alone, so I would have needed to say “both”. I also didn’t want to copy someone else’s design, so I dropped that idea.

Instead I asked for an original thumbnail showing off Images 2.5 and gave it the announcement link for context. After some tweaking, it kept my face consistent with my photo and drew the OpenAI logo, which earlier versions sometimes struggled with. It had too much text, so I said “make this simpler with less text and keep the overall design”, and it did exactly that. My favourite version was a before-and-after of me. Zoomed in, it was a little blurry, but fine for a thumbnail. From there I usually take thumbnails into VidIQ for further tweaks.

FAQ

Do I need a polished drawing?

No. My windows, chair and dog were very rough and the layout still came through. Clear placement and relative size matter more than drawing skill.

Will an edit preserve everything else?

Not reliably. OpenAI’s help article says highlights are not always precise and edits may extend beyond the selected area. Compare the whole image with the previous version after every change.

Where is Sketch in ChatGPT?

OpenAI’s current help article documents it in the mobile app: type @ in the message box and select Sketch, draw, confirm with the checkmark, then add instructions and send.

Can ChatGPT pull a YouTube thumbnail from a link?

It didn’t work when I tried it. ChatGPT couldn’t fetch the thumbnail from the URL, so I uploaded the image file instead, which worked.
