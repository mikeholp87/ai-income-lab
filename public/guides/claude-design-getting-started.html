Quick answer

On 22 April 2026 I opened Claude Design, five days after the 17 April launch, uploaded a TubeAnalytics screenshot, and asked for a 10-slide investor pitch. I answered pre-seed, $1 to $3 million, angels, bold tone, and a creators-as-media-companies story. It wrote HTML slides with speaker notes in a few minutes. I switched from Opus 4.7 to Sonnet 4.6 to save credits, then exported a PDF. TubeAnalytics is my own product. This is the first-look deck, not a full site redesign.

TubeAnalytics is my own SaaS. Captions that say TubeBuddy Analytics mean TubeAnalytics. Claude Design was a research preview for Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise on that launch. I was out of Claude Pro credits and it still ran. A later site-redesign pass is Redesign a Site in Claude Design. Open Design on OpenCode is a different tool: Open Design with OpenCode.

1. Open Claude Design

The product page from the description is claude.ai/design/. I said it rolled out on 17 April 2026, used Claude Opus 4.7, and made designs, prototypes, slides, and one-pagers. News I repeated that day said Figma stock dropped about 7 to 8 percent. I did not verify that figure myself. The UI offered a new prototype, slide deck, template, project, or design system. Prototype let me pick wireframe or high-fidelity.

2. Upload a screenshot and answer questions

I uploaded a TubeAnalytics screenshot and typed:

create a slide deck investor pitch presentation.

It asked qualifying questions. I answered round pre-seed, raise $1 to $3 million, audience angels, 10 slides, tone bold and confident, narrative creators becoming media companies, and competitors that included View Stats. Continue started the build. Those raise figures were answers for the demo, not a live fundraise.

3. Edit, switch models, and export

It wrote JavaScript in a couple of seconds, then an HTML pitch with 10 slides, a mock-up, and speaker notes. I wanted red to match the logo. Text and design were editable. An overflowing slide was auto-redesigned. Export options were HTML download, skills including animate video, PowerPoint, PDF, HTML, send to Canva, and GitHub. I did not connect GitHub. On Opus 4.7 I switched to Sonnet 4.6 because Opus burns credits. Full-screen present worked. After edits I had a small logo, updated colors, and a techy font closer to the site. Graphs still needed work. I said it was not yet a Canva competitor.

Done when

  • Opened Claude Design
  • Generated a deck from a screenshot and the qualifying answers
  • Exported PDF, PowerPoint, or HTML and reviewed the slides

What I didn't cover

  • A production-ready pitch. I said the deck still needed quite a bit of work.
  • Credit prices. I was already out of Pro credits and still ran the preview.
  • The Figma stock move. I repeated news, not a check of the chart.
