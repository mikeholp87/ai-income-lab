Codex usage limits: check usage, plans and reset times

Quick answer

Codex usage limits depend on your plan and workload; check Settings → Usage or /status in the CLI for remaining allowance and reset times, then use the continuation options available to your account (OpenAI guidance).

A Codex usage limit is easier to diagnose when you separate three questions: which account is signed in, which allowance is exhausted, and when that allowance refreshes. This guide explains that check before you pay for more usage. It also separates current documentation from the plan changes shown in the recorded walkthrough.

Official sources checked as of October 31, 2026. Account-specific offers and allowances can change, so check the linked pages before changing plans.

How Codex usage limits work by ChatGPT plan

OpenAI publishes estimates, not a universal number of tasks you can finish. Its guidance says the model, task and settings affect consumption. Where five-hour and weekly windows both apply, you need remaining allowance in both. Pro currently has no five-hour limit, but still has an included allowance.

Do not translate a plan name into “unlimited coding.” Compare your actual workload with the allowance shown in your account. A short correction and an open-ended investigation are different jobs; record what you asked for before comparing two sessions.

Local messages and cloud chats share the plan allowance, so moving a task to the cloud does not create a separate quota. OpenAI also says weekly limits may apply. Source: Codex pricing.

Codex plans (including family plan)

OpenAI’s Codex pricing page says Codex is included in the ChatGPT Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise plans. Free and Go get limited access. Codex Cloud is available to eligible Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, Healthcare and Education accounts, and is not included with Free or Go. Source: using Codex with your plan.

There is no ChatGPT family plan on OpenAI’s pricing page as of October 31, 2026. The pricing FAQ says Free, Go and Plus are designed for individuals, while Business and Enterprise are for businesses, with Business starting at 2 users. If several people need Codex, check those plans on the pricing page rather than relying on a family discount that OpenAI does not list. This guide gives no prices because they vary by plan and region. Source: ChatGPT pricing.

Codex usage dashboard: where to see remaining usage

Open Settings or the Codex usage dashboard and check the exhausted allowance, available credit balance and displayed reset time. During an active CLI session, enter /status. These are OpenAI’s documented places to inspect the limit. Source: using Codex with your plan.

In the desktop app, the dashboard lives at Settings > Usage & billing. On the web it is Usage settings. The mobile app does not have it. Settings > Usage & billing also has a History view under Usage limit resets that shows banked reset activity from the past 30 days. In the CLI, enter /status during an active session, or enter /usage and select Redeem reset to use a banked reset if your version shows that option. Usage can take a short time to update after a reset, so refresh before assuming something is wrong. Source: how resets work in Codex.

  1. Write down the exact limit message and the time you saw it.
  2. Check the account and workspace you intended to use.
  3. Read each allowance separately rather than relying on one percentage.
  4. Keep the displayed reset time with your task notes so you can plan the next attempt.

If the screen seems inconsistent, avoid guessing from a previous screenshot. For a support request, collect the plan, task reference, date, timezone, model, reasoning level and speed setting. OpenAI asks for these details when investigating unexpected usage. Source: usage troubleshooting.

It complements the Codex dashboard and does not replace it: your plan’s remaining allowance and reset time still come from Codex.

Codex quota: what counts and when it resets

Your Codex quota is the included allowance on your plan. On plans that use shared allowances or credits, Codex, ChatGPT Work, ChatGPT for Excel and Workspace Agents draw from the same pool. How fast it goes down depends on the model, where the task runs, task complexity, context, reasoning, speed and tools. Auto-review safety checks are free when you sign in with ChatGPT. ChatGPT image, file upload and regular voice limits are separate and do not apply to Codex. Source: using Codex with your plan.

The main allowances differ by plan, according to OpenAI’s reset guide:

  • Go: a monthly allowance.
  • Plus and Business Standard: a 5-hour and a weekly allowance.
  • Pro 100, Pro 200 and Pro 500: a weekly allowance.

Reset dates are not fixed to your billing date. A banked, global or paid reset can restart a usage period, and the new schedule replaces the old one. If a weekly period due to end on Friday restarts on Tuesday, the next refresh is the following Tuesday. Check Usage for your next refresh time instead of calculating it yourself, because the displayed time can be an estimate before a new period starts. OpenAI Support does not reset usage limits. Source: how resets work in Codex.

What to do when a limit hits

First preserve your work: note completed edits, checks already run and the next unfinished step. Then choose a continuation path. OpenAI says an active turn may continue after a limit is reached, subject to fair-use limits; afterward, the usage page or banner shows the available choices. Source: reaching a limit during work.

Wait for the scheduled reset

Use the reset time shown for the exhausted allowance. On plans with both windows, the weekly and five-hour periods can end at different times; a refreshed weekly allowance does not necessarily mean the shorter window has refreshed. OpenAI’s reset guide explains this distinction.

Use the pause to inspect the diff, reproduce the error yourself or write a clearer next brief. That gives the next session a concrete starting point. Repeatedly sending the same broad request is unlikely to teach you which part of the task actually needs attention.

Review a plan change or eligible extra usage

The limit banner may offer an upgrade or additional credits. Availability depends on your account. On eligible Pro features, included usage is consumed before purchased credits; balances and purchases appear in Settings → Usage or Codex Usage & Billing. Source: Pro usage and billing. For credits and Pro tier details, read the existing plan and credit explainer.

A banked reset, if your account has one, is another option. Review its scope and expiry before redeeming it. Promotional resets are discretionary, and selected accounts may see paid instant resets; neither permanently raises the plan’s allowance. Source: reset types and eligibility.

Use separately billed API access for local work

OpenAI documents API-key sign-in for local work in the desktop app, CLI and IDE extension. This uses API billing through the OpenAI Platform account instead of included ChatGPT usage. Cloud Codex requires ChatGPT sign-in. In the CLI, switch with printenv OPENAI_API_KEY | codex login --with-api-key and confirm the active method with codex login status. Decide what you are willing to spend first. An API key is a different billing path, not a subscription reset.

The recorded sign-in fix: April 2026

In my Codex usage limits: the recorded quota walkthrough, I hit my weekly limit and started a ChatGPT Business trial. The CLI still showed the old plan. Signing out and signing back in with the Business account was the fix that worked in that recording.

The plan details describe April 2026, when I recorded this. My video can't tell you today’s trial availability or price, and it doesn't promise more weekly work. The useful troubleshooting lesson is to verify which account the client is using after a plan change.

For a current authentication check, OpenAI documents codex login status; codex logout clears stored credentials, and codex login starts the ChatGPT sign-in flow. These commands concern authentication. Do not treat signing in again as a quota-reset method. Source: authentication and sign-out.

Practical ways to stretch your allowance

OpenAI recommends narrower prompts, relevant source files, appropriately sized output, smaller project instructions and fewer unnecessary MCP servers. Source: Codex pricing documentation. Put that advice into a brief you can check:

  • Name the finish line. Ask for one broken behavior to be fixed, with a specific check showing it works.
  • Point to relevant files. Include the failing command and error rather than asking for an unexplained review of everything.
  • Separate required work from suggestions. Ask for optional improvements in the final notes instead of adding them to the implementation.
  • Keep instructions useful. Remove stale project guidance and repeated background that does not help this task.

For routine work, OpenAI recommends a lower-cost model. Source: making usage last. Try that on a task with an obvious acceptance check, then inspect the result. There is no promised savings percentage in this guide.

Reasoning, context, tools and speed affect usage. Fast and Ultrafast consume included allowance faster than Standard for the same model. Source: different usage for similar tasks. Choose settings deliberately instead of assuming the most intensive option is necessary for every edit.

The Codex workflow guide gives you a small change to practice: brief it, inspect the diff and verify the result. Keep your own record of useful completed tasks, interruptions and rework. That is a better basis for your next subscription decision than copying someone else’s busiest day.

FAQ

Where can I check my remaining Codex usage?

Open Settings → Usage or the Codex usage dashboard. In an active CLI session, enter /status to see remaining limits.

Reference: OpenAI documentation.

Does Codex have a weekly limit?

Weekly limits may apply. Where both five-hour and weekly limits apply, you need allowance remaining in both windows.

Reference: OpenAI documentation.

Does starting a new chat reset the limit?

No. OpenAI says starting a new Codex chat does not reset usage limits.

Reference: OpenAI documentation.

Can I keep working after my included allowance runs out?

Check your limit banner for available options, such as waiting, upgrading, adding credits or applying an available reset. API-key access for local work is billed separately.

Reference: OpenAI documentation and continuation options.

Will signing out and back in give me more usage?

Not that I saw. In my April video, signing out and back in only fixed the CLI showing my old plan after I upgraded. It never renewed a used-up allowance, so don't count on it for that.

Reference: OpenAI documentation and the recorded walkthrough.
