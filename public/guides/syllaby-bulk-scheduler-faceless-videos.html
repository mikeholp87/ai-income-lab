Quick answer

In Syllaby create a faceless video, choose a style, subtitles and voice, then use the bulk option: it generates scripts for 7 to 30 days from a few prompts, renders each video in about 5 to 10 minutes and places them in the content calendar with posts scheduled to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and other accounts, up to five a day. I quoted 13 credits per minute of video and 500 credits a month on the paid plan, which starts at $25 a month. The script assistant failed with not-found errors several times before it worked.

This is an August 2025 tutorial after about three months of using Syllaby for faceless videos on YouTube. I said I was seeing pretty good results but showed no numbers here. The Syllaby link is an affiliate link. For a single first video, start with the Syllaby first test.

Which faceless-video path? Syllaby for the fastest first publish; Make + JSON2Video for spreadsheet-driven batches; HeyGen + ElevenLabs when you want an avatar and voice clone (and to see the API plan wall).

Prices and plan names change. Confirm them on the vendor page before you buy. Checked November 15, 2026 against the Sources links below where listed.

1. What Syllaby covers

The dashboard turns text into video, makes B-roll videos and converts product pages into video ads. There is a gallery of examples and an analytics tab with views per video. Videos and assets list all videos by status (draft, processing, completed, published) and can publish to TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram and Threads. It also has AI avatars and voice clones, which I covered elsewhere with HeyGen and ElevenLabs.

2. One faceless video

Choose Create video, then Faceless video. I usually pick hyper realism, then adjust the subtitle style, voice and aspect ratio. ElevenLabs voices can also be connected. I quoted 13 credits per minute of video and 500 credits a month on the paid plan. You can adjust animation, sound effects, background music and subtitle position before generating.

3. Script assistant and calendar

The AI script assistant gives video ideas and scripts. It returned not-found errors several times in my walkthrough before it worked, so retry before assuming your input is wrong. You can then schedule a post from the content calendar.

4. Bulk schedule

The bulk option creates any number of videos from about a week to a month from a few prompts. I chose hyper realism, customised the subtitles, picked an American voice and generated the scripts, which took a few seconds. After reading and editing them, Generate video renders each one and takes about 5 to 10 minutes. They land in the content calendar and posting is scheduled for you, with up to five posts a day.

I also said that for monetization, long-form videos matter more than Shorts, which pay little unless views reach the millions. Shorts here are for reach.
