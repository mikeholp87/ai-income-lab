Quick answer

Both workflows scrape remote digital marketing jobs from Indeed with an Apify actor and have OpenAI judge each job against your resume, then draft documents only for good matches and log every job. The Airtable version runs every three days, scores jobs out of 10 with GPT-5 mini and writes a cover letter for scores of 5 or more into a Google Doc. The Google Sheets version loops over jobs in batches of five with GPT-4.1, writes a tailored resume with GPT-4.1 mini and logs matches in a sheet. Applying stays manual, and one live run stopped on Google authentication errors.

These are two November 2025 builds that I use for remote job hunting. They send your resume and job descriptions to an AI service, so keep private details out of the resume you share and check the provider’s data policy. They draft documents but do not apply for you, which I do by hand. Apify gives $5 of free monthly credit on its free plan, and the Apify link here is an affiliate link. For Apify basics, see the Apify agent skills guide.

1. Scrape the jobs

A schedule trigger runs every three days at 8 a.m. in the Airtable version; the Sheets version starts from a manual trigger. The Apify Indeed job scraper takes a country and a query: I searched the United States for remote digital marketing roles, limited to 25 jobs from the past week. Apify also has actors for LinkedIn, Google Jobs, Glassdoor and others. An n8n step then fetches the dataset items with description, company, location and salary.

2. Score the fit

In the Airtable build, GPT-5 mini scores each job out of 10 against my resume, giving points for resume fit, pay, remote work, benefits and full-time status. An if branch keeps scores of 5 or more. In the Sheets build, GPT-4.1 judges each job in batches of five and a filter keeps the good fits. The resume is read from a Google Doc, which stopped my live run with an authentication error.

3. Draft the documents

For good matches the Airtable build writes a cover letter from the listing and my resume, then creates a Google Doc and updates it with the text. The Sheets build writes a tailored resume with GPT-4.1 mini as HTML for easy copying, converts it and creates a Google Doc with a sharing permission. Cover letter files can be hard to match to jobs when a listing has no company name, so I search Drive for a likely one.

4. Track and apply

Every job goes into an Airtable table with its rating or a Google Sheet, so I can open a link and apply by hand. The Airtable version recorded all jobs, including low scores; the Sheets version logged matches.

Done when

  • Apify Indeed actor on a schedule
  • OpenAI rates fit out of 10
  • Cover letter or resume, log in Airtable or Sheets

What I didn't cover

FAQ

Does it apply for jobs?

No. It finds, scores and drafts. I apply by hand.

Which score counts as a good match?

In the Airtable version, 5 out of 10 or higher.

Can it scrape other boards?

Apify has actors for LinkedIn, Google Jobs, Glassdoor and more. I only ran Indeed.
