Gamma for a Conference Workshop: Deck, Site and PDF Export

Quick answer

Ask ChatGPT for an outline of your talk, then in Gamma choose Create new, paste the text, pick Presentation and the default fluid format and generate from notes. I set concise text for a 40-minute workshop, described the audience and tone, picked a professional theme with automatic images and watched the slides build in real time. I then improved the writing, restyled the deck, added a slide comparing n8n and Make.com, published it as a site and exported a PDF because the Google Slides export failed.

This is an October 2025 build for an AI workshop I was giving at a digital nomad conference in Bangkok the next month. I wanted to avoid building every slide by hand, and I found Gamma by asking Google Gemini for AI presentation tools. The Gamma link is an affiliate link. For a client coaching deck made from a pasted plan, see the Gamma coaching deck guide.

Prices and plan names change. Confirm them on the vendor page before you buy. Checked November 15, 2026 against the Sources links below where listed.

1. Find the tool and paste the outline

Gemini suggested Gamma and Beautiful.ai. Beautiful.ai had only a free trial, while Gamma was free to start, so I used Gamma. In Gamma choose Create new, then paste text, generate from a one-line prompt or import a PDF or PowerPoint. I pasted the ChatGPT outline, chose Presentation, kept the default fluid format (16 by 9 and 4 by 3 are also offered) and chose Generate from notes or an outline.

2. Set text, audience and theme

The text amount options are minimal, concise, detailed and extensive. The talk was about 40 minutes with a Q&A, so I chose concise. I wrote the audience and tone, picked a professional theme with automatic images and generated the deck. The slides appeared in real time.

3. Revise with AI

Improve writing rewrote the whole deck, and a theme suggestion restyled it. I also asked Gamma to add a slide comparing n8n and Make.com. It listed n8n as open source, self-hostable and developer friendly with lower long-term cost at high volume, and Make.com as cloud-based with a visual drag-and-drop interface and higher cost but immediate deployment. For my own comparison of the two, see n8n vs Zapier vs Make.

4. Publish or export

Share offers PDF, PowerPoint, Google Slides or images, and installing fonts helps PowerPoint look right. Hiding the Gamma logo needs a paid plan, about $10 a month as I said then. You can also convert the deck to a site and publish it to its own Gamma address; a custom domain needs a paid plan. I published the site so I could send it to the organizers.

The Google Slides export did not work in my walkthrough after the sign-in step, so I exported a PDF. It looked good out of the box, with some revision still needed.

FAQ

Is Gamma free?

Free to start in my walkthrough, with paid plans from about $10 a month at the time. Check current pricing.

Can Gamma make a website?

Yes. You can convert a deck to a site and publish it. A custom domain needs a paid plan.

Why did you export a PDF?

The Google Slides export did not work in my test, so I used PDF instead.
