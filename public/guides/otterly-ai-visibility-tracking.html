1. Open the brand report

Otterly tracks prompts and volume, then an overview brand report versus competitors. Spoken TubeAnalytics figures that day: brand coverage about 43 percent versus VidIQ about 68 percent, about 20 brand mentions, roughly the same as some competitors, brand position slightly above VidIQ and below Outlier Kit and TubeBuddy, brand ranking position number four above ViewStats, a visibility index that called us a leader in brand coverage above TubeBuddy, and likelihood to buy around 78 percent. Citations were beating many competitors without backlinks, with a citation count even above youtube.com on that view.

2. Crawlability and content checks

The crawlability checker on an article showed all crawlers allowed via robots.txt. The content checker covered static versus dynamic readiness, structured data, and section breakdown such as quick answer and how-to. It suggested telling Claude Code to implement each item. Reports were downloadable.

3. Prompt tracking and citation ideas

Prompt tracking listed citations, domain citations, and brand sentiment. Opening a prompt showed ChatGPT-style answers and sources, with my blog in almost every one. One prompt maxed out at 100. Another, how can I track up to 20 YouTube competitors, beat most metrics except sources, where TubeBuddy won. A tool-to-compare-competitor-channels prompt had me at 67 out of 100 at the top. Citation opportunities included a Sprout Social article with a 90 domain rating, where I could pitch a link exchange because I had a compare article. Recommendations let me select all prompts and view suggested fixes.

Done when

  • Opened an Otterly brand report for a domain you control
  • Read coverage, mentions, and citations against named competitors
  • Checked crawlability and at least one tracked prompt

What I didn't cover

  • Account-creation fields. I was already logged in.
  • Proof that Otterly caused those citation counts. I only read the dashboard.
  • A GEOSEO GitHub tutorial. I said I would make one later.
