Quick answer

If you want to build AI workflows, start with the free vendor academies: Make Academy for Make.com and the n8n Academy for n8n both teach their own tools at no cost. Use Zapier’s guides if you are on Zapier, and DeepLearning.AI if you want AI engineering fundamentals. Pay for a course or community only when you want feedback, templates or live help. I run AI Income Lab, a paid Skool community, so I list it last and say where the free options are the better choice.

Who this is for: Anyone choosing how to learn AI workflows with Make.com, n8n or Zapier, who wants to know when free options are enough.

People ask me which course to take to learn AI automation. The honest answer depends on the tool you will use and whether you need feedback or only lessons. I checked each provider’s public pages on November 18, 2026, and I have not taken their paid courses, so this compares what they say they offer, not how good the teaching is. I run AI Income Lab, so I am not neutral. The video below builds an n8n workflow that adds new Airtable contacts to an email platform, and my introduction lesson for a Make.com course shows how I teach inside the community. Its description has an affiliate link for Make.com.

1. Decide what you need first

Four questions narrow the field quickly. Which tool will you use, Make.com, n8n, Zapier or code? Do you want self-paced lessons or someone to answer your questions? Do you need templates you can copy, or are you happy to build from scratch? And what will you pay, in money and in time? Most people can answer the first question in a minute, and that alone points to a free academy.

2. Free academies from the tools themselves

Make Academy describes itself as a free online path for learning Make, with hands-on courses, badges and progress tracking. When I looked it listed a learning path called Automation to AI Agents: Advanced, estimated at about 3 hours. The n8n Academy at learn.n8n.io offers free registration and courses including N8N101 Essentials: Your First Workflows, N8N102 Integrations: APIs and Connected Workflows, N8N103 In Practice: AI, Testing and Best Practices, and a Quickstart. Both are the best place to learn their own product in depth, and both are tied to that one tool.

3. Zapier’s learning resources

Zapier publishes guides on automation, AI strategy, lead management and marketing, and it describes its platform as connecting 9,000+ apps. They suit you if you already use Zapier. If you want a tool-neutral view or you plan to use Make or n8n, look at the academies above or at my guide to what AI automation is, which compares tools with a first build.

4. DeepLearning.AI, Coursera and Maven

DeepLearning.AI describes its catalogue as AI courses, specializations and short courses, with membership plans and learning tracks. It leans towards AI and machine-learning skills more than drag-and-drop automation. Coursera lists courses, Professional Certificates, Specializations and degrees from universities and companies, including DeepLearning.AI. Maven describes live courses taught by working experts in areas that include AI. For any listing here, check the tools it teaches, when it was last updated and what it costs, because AI courses go out of date quickly.

5. Learning to code with AI

freeCodeCamp offers a free coding curriculum, which is a different goal from building with AI coding agents. I cover that second goal in the Claude Code tutorial and in building apps with AI coding agents. If you want to know whether you need coding at all, the AI automation guide shows what no-code tools can do on their own.

6. Where AI Income Lab fits, and where it does not

AI Income Lab is a paid Skool community I host, at $29, $49 and $89 a month. It includes a Beginner’s Automation Course, a VAPI AI Voice Agent Course and an AI Avatar Video Course, a library of 6,400+ n8n templates on VIP, weekly group coaching on VIP and a community of 2,900+ members. It is a better fit than the free academies when you want templates, someone to ask while you build and courses across several tools. It is a worse fit when you only need one tool’s official lessons or a recognised certificate, since the vendor academies and DeepLearning.AI do that at lower or no cost. The Make.com introduction lesson linked above is public, so you can judge my teaching style before you pay anything.

FAQ

What is the best free way to learn Make.com?

Make Academy is the vendor’s own free learning path with hands-on courses and badges, according to its site.

What is the best free way to learn n8n?

The n8n Academy at learn.n8n.io offers free registration and courses from a first workflow through AI, testing and best practices.

Do I need to pay for an AI workflow course?

Not to start. Free academies and my own guides cover the basics. Pay when you want feedback, templates or live help.

How is AI Income Lab different from the free academies?

It adds courses across several tools, a library of 6,400+ n8n templates on VIP and weekly group coaching on VIP. The academies are better for official, tool-specific depth.
