Quick answer

In Claude Desktop click the plus icon, choose Add a connector and enter the vidIQ MCP server URL from vidIQ’s MCP page. Then ask for a video idea validation, a full channel audit or a competitor breakdown. Claude calls vidIQ tools for keyword research, outliers and trending videos and answers from real channel data. In my May 2026 test the channel health check first returned the wrong channel, and pasting the channel ID fixed it.

Prices and plan names change. Confirm them on the vendor page before you buy. Checked November 15, 2026.

This follows a May 2026 walkthrough. vidIQ did not sponsor the video, and the vidIQ link on this page is an affiliate link. Also note that I am building a competing product, TubeAnalytics, which I mention in the video. Paid vidIQ plans started at $19 a month then, so check its page. For a broader channel process, see the YouTube channel growth workflow.

1. Add the connector

vidIQ says its MCP works with the Claude web app, Claude Desktop, Cursor, ChatGPT, Codex and other MCP clients. I used Claude Desktop. Click the plus icon, choose Add a connector and, because it usually will not be in the list, paste the MCP server URL from vidIQ’s MCP page. It then appears as a vidIQ connector. For what MCP is, see What is MCP?.

2. Validate a video idea

The connector offers starter prompts: content gap analysis, competitor breakdown, channel health check, niche evaluation and video idea validation. I ran idea validation on the phrase “vidIQ MCP”. Claude ran keyword research and reported that outlier results were unrelated videos, which it read as almost no video supply for that exact phrase, then checked trending and breakout sorts. You can open the requests to see which tools it called.

3. Audit a channel

A full audit of my channel returned subscribers, total views, videos published, channel age and a 90-day growth picture, plus upload cadence (about four videos a week), average views and subscribers per video, and view velocity at 1, 24 and 48 hours. It also suggested keyword opportunities and which of my topics were performing well.

The first health check pulled the wrong channel. I pasted the channel ID into the prompt and the competitor breakdown then worked, with a 30-day snapshot and keyword opportunities.

4. Read the answers critically

One note said my TubeAnalytics video had almost no genuine community response and looked seeded. That was right in a way: I paid for YouTube ads on that video. Another note said my tags were inconsistent, and I disagreed because I tag with vidIQ. The tool reads signals; it does not know why. Treat every finding as a prompt to check.
