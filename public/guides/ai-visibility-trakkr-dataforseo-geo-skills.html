AI Search Visibility: Trakkr, DataForSEO MCP and GEO Skills

Quick answer

I use Trakkr to see how AI answer engines cite my site, how often AI crawlers visit and which prompts mention my brand, and its MCP gives Codex the same data to find citation gaps. A GEO blog-writer skill then drafted a 16-article cluster with quick answers, key takeaways, tables, sources and schema. Separately, the free DataForSEO MCP in Claude Code produced keyword data and an SEO audit that Codex implemented, with desktop Lighthouse performance rising. My numbers are self-reported, and I cannot prove the articles or the Vercel move caused the visits.

This case study merges three videos from April and May 2026 about my own YouTube analytics SaaS. Figures such as about 1,700 AI crawler visits in a week, 10,000 to 15,000 visits a day at the peak and showing in roughly 92 percent of tracked prompts come from my dashboards. Trakkr and DataForSEO links are affiliate links. For the Trakkr and Codex workflow in detail, see Trakkr and Codex.

1. What Trakkr shows (April 2026)

After entering my domain, the dashboard showed a visibility score, mentions, rank, citations and AI traffic, with competitors such as Social Blade, vidIQ and ViewStats listed. I ranked about fifth. AI crawler visits were around 1,700 in seven days, mostly from ChatGPT, with top pages and sources by day. I tested prompts about the best YouTube analytics tools in ChatGPT and follow-up questions on pricing and features in Perplexity, where my site ranked third. Trakkr offered a free trial. I also installed its snippet directly or through Google Tag Manager.

2. GEO content and files

My blog articles open with a quick answer, then key takeaways, structured data, fact-style sections and an expanded FAQ so crawlers can read them. I also keep llms.txt and ai.txt files that list pages and allow AI crawling. I submit URLs to Bing and Google with custom scripts; a paid tool, Index Guru, costs about 9 a month. I credited GEO-written articles and moving from Hostinger to Vercel for part of the rise, but I did not isolate causes.

3. Trakkr MCP in Codex (May 2026)

Trakkr added an MCP server. I pasted its config into Codex and asked for citation trends, top-cited pages and competitor gaps where others were cited and I was not. I installed the GEO content optimizer skill with the skills command and had it write batches of articles, then planned a 16-article cluster and a second round aimed at the gaps. I did not show whether those articles earned more citations. I covered a related skills approach in the OpenCode skills guide.

4. DataForSEO MCP in Claude Code (April 2026)

For data-driven SEO I created a DataForSEO account, copied the API key, cloned the free MCP from GitHub, ran npm install and added a .env file, then started Claude Code with the MCP. I asked for search volume for the top keywords my domain ranks for, approving the ranked-keywords tool. The LLM mentions check needed a 14-day trial and returned no tracked mentions, which I said was wrong. Claude wrote an SEO audit report, an implementation summary, a keyword map and a priority list. Codex then implemented fixes such as less DOM bloat, product schema and fewer render-blocking resources, and Lighthouse showed desktop performance up. Rankings were not shown, and the audit suggested nothing I verified independently.

What I didn't cover

  • Rankings after the DataForSEO-driven fixes.
  • Cost of Trakkr and DataForSEO beyond the trials.

FAQ

Does Trakkr have a free trial?

Yes, a trial in each video; plans change, so check its page.

What is GEO?

Generative engine optimization: writing and structuring pages so AI answer engines cite them.

Are the numbers independent?

No. They are from my own dashboards and I did not isolate causes.
