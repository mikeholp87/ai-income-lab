ChatGPT Pro 20x: why the $200 plan was paused, and what changed since

Quick answer: as of October 22, 2026, ChatGPT Pro 20x (Pro 200) is available again at $200/month, with a lower allowance for new subscribers.

ChatGPT Pro 20x is the $200 per month tier of ChatGPT Pro, which OpenAI now calls Pro 200. In September 2026 OpenAI stopped selling it to new subscribers because GPT-6 Astra demand outran capacity, and the pricing page offered only the 5x plan at about $100 per month. People who already had 20x kept it. Since then the plan has come back with a smaller allowance. This page covers what happened, what OpenAI’s own pages say today, how Plus, 5x and 20x compare, and how Codex credits fit in. (Searching for “ChatGPT 20x” or “Codex 20x”? Both mean this same Pro 200 plan.)

Status as of October 22, 2026

  • Paused: new Pro 20x ($200/month) subscriptions were paused around September 10–11, 2026. My video was published on September 11.
  • Back: OpenAI’s help page now says: “Pro 200 is also available for new subscriptions again. New subscriptions that aren’t eligible for grandfathering include a lower usage allowance.”
  • Price: “The monthly price remains $200.”
  • Existing subscribers: if you had an active Pro 200 subscription at any point from September 22 through 10 a.m. Pacific Time on September 29, 2026, you “can use the previous included allowance through October 29, 2026.” After that date, your subscription moves to the lower allowance.
  • New tier: OpenAI added Pro 500 at $500/month, the only Pro plan with Ultrafast.

Source: About ChatGPT Pro tiers (OpenAI Help Center), read October 22, 2026. OpenAI doesn’t give the new allowance as a multiplier, so this page doesn’t either. Reports of a specific new figure on Reddit and X are unconfirmed.

The short answer (what the video covers)

  • What paused: new subscriptions and upgrades to Pro 20x ($200/month). Only the 5x plan (about $100/month) could be bought.
  • Existing 20x users: kept access. OpenAI said there was no impact on existing users.
  • Why: demand for GPT-6 Astra. People were using up the $200 plan so fast that OpenAI stopped new sign-ups while it added capacity.
  • For how long: then, unknown. The wording was that new Pro subscriptions might be paused “for a bit.” Now, it’s back for new subscribers, per OpenAI’s help center (October 22, 2026).

Why OpenAI paused it

I moved from Plus, which I’d used for about a year and a half, to Pro because Plus wasn’t enough for what I wanted to do with GPT-6 Astra. On Plus my credits were gone in about 5 to 10 minutes of Astra. Even on Pro I used up my plan in about 5 hours. Astra uses credits faster than any model I’ve tried.

The OpenAI person who handles usage credit resets (Tibo on X) posted that demand for Astra was unprecedented. OpenAI was pulling every lever it could. Existing users came first, so it might have to pause new Pro subscriptions for a bit. In the video, that tweet showed 8.2 million views. A post by Jeff Hunter on Facebook also reported the pause. He wrote that he spent about $1,200 across all his subscriptions to run 50+ agents a day, and he estimated the same work would cost about $3,000 a day on Claude Fable.

The September 2026 timeline

As reported in Jeff Hunter’s Facebook post shown in the video (not confirmed by OpenAI, with dates as he gave them). He published the same timeline in his write-up on the pause:

  1. September 3: Astra launched as a restricted preview. Paying subscribers saw the announcement but couldn’t use the model yet.
  2. September 4: Sam Altman reportedly said Pro, Enterprise and Business Premium had access. Plus users were told he hoped they’d get it that weekend, but it wasn’t promised.
  3. September 5: OpenAI started giving subscribers one banked usage reset for each day they went without Astra.
  4. September 7: a global usage reset. One developer used up the whole $200 plan in about 12 hours.
  5. September 10: the $200 Pro plan was paused for new subscribers, at least for a while.

Codex limits and credits on Pro 20x, 5x and Plus

For the account checks, reset windows and next steps when a limit hits, read the Codex usage limits guide.

If you mainly use Codex (the “Codex 20x” question), what matters is how much coding work you get before you hit a limit. OpenAI’s Codex pricing page says “Local messages and cloud chats share your plan’s usage allowance. Weekly limits may also apply.” So the 5-hour window isn’t the only cap.

When you run out, credits are the next step. In OpenAI’s words, “Credits let you use Codex beyond the usage included in your plan. Credit usage varies by model and task.” The Pro tiers page adds that “Included usage is used first, followed by your credit balance.” You can check your balance in Settings → Usage in ChatGPT, or Usage & Billing in Codex. In the CLI, /status shows your remaining limits during a session. Support can’t reset limits for you.

Two settings change how fast you burn through usage. Per the same docs, Fast mode uses your included allowance at 2.5x the Standard rate (2x when it draws on purchased credits), and Ultrafast, only on Pro 500, uses it at 8x (6x on credits). On Plus, Astra is limited and credits are how you get more. Credits are charged per token. At Standard speed, OpenAI lists GPT-6 Astra at “250 credits” per million input tokens and “1,250 credits” per million output tokens, against 50 and 250 for GPT-6.1 Sol (ChatGPT Learn: Pricing, read October 22, 2026). That’s why Astra drains credits so fast, and why moving routine tasks to a cheaper model makes them last. The page says the price you pay for credits depends on your plan, so I don’t list a dollar figure.

The video also covers usage resets. The OpenAI person who handles them (Tibo on X) has been generous with them, including for Codex. For a walkthrough of raising Codex limits, watch Here’s How To 10X Your Credit Limit On Codex!

Make your credits last: use medium effort

Pick the reasoning effort yourself. In Codex I use medium:

  • Medium: the best balance for most work, including agentic coding, research and multi-step tasks.
  • High: for hard debugging or long tasks.
  • Extra high and max: avoid them for routine work. You get less back for the extra effort and the model tends to over-engineer. Save them for audit-level tasks or for failures that lower levels can’t fix.

FAQ

Why was ChatGPT Pro 20x paused?

In September 2026, demand for GPT-6 Astra outran OpenAI’s capacity, so it paused new sign-ups to the $200 plan.

I already pay for Pro 20x. Do I lose it?

No. Existing subscribers kept access when sales were paused in September 2026. As of October 22, 2026, OpenAI’s help center says eligible existing Pro 200 subscribers keep the previous included allowance through October 29, 2026, then move to the lower allowance. The $200 price stays the same.

Which Pro plan could you still buy?

During the September 2026 pause, the 5x plan at about $100 per month. People on Plus or Pro 5x could no longer upgrade to 20x.

How many messages does each plan get?

In September 2026, per the video, each 5-hour window gave Plus about 5 to 45 messages, Pro $100 about 25 to 225, and Pro 20x about 100 to 900. OpenAI’s pricing docs now say “Pro plans currently have no five-hour limit,” though weekly limits may apply.

Is ChatGPT Pro 20x available again?

Yes. As of October 22, 2026, OpenAI’s help center says Pro 200 is available for new subscriptions again at $200 a month. New subscribers who don’t qualify for grandfathering get a lower usage allowance than before. When I recorded the video on September 11, nobody knew how long the pause would last.

How do Codex credits work?

Your plan’s included usage is used first, then your credit balance. Credits cover eligible Codex usage beyond your plan, and how fast they go depends on the model and task. Plus has limited GPT-6 Astra usage in Codex and can buy credits for more. Check your balance under Settings → Usage, or with /status in the CLI.

How do I make my credits last longer?

Use medium reasoning effort for most work. Save high for hard debugging, and avoid extra high and max for routine tasks.
