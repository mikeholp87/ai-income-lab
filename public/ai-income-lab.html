AI Income Lab: AI workflow courses, templates and coaching

Quick answer: AI Income Lab is the paid learning community for AI Automation Station, hosted on Skool by me, Mike Holp. It gives you step-by-step courses on automation, voice agents and AI avatar video, a library of 6,400+ n8n templates on the top tier, weekly group coaching on VIP and a community of 2,900+ members. Plans are $29, $49 and $89 a month, and every video and guide on this site stays free.

Member count and prices are from the Skool page as of November 2026. Check the current plans on Skool before you join.

Who it is for

  • You want to build AI workflows with n8n or Make.com and prefer a guided path to piecing together free videos.
  • You want to build an AI voice agent or an AI avatar video, with a course that walks through setup and testing.
  • You want a place to ask questions while you build, and weekly group help if you choose VIP.
  • You are building automations for your own business or for clients and want templates to adapt.

Who it is not for

  • You only want the free material. Every written guide and video is free without a Skool login.
  • You want a custom build done for you. Membership does not include custom business implementation.
  • You want a guaranteed income. Results depend on your project, your experience and the time you put in.

Templates and a learner community

If you want an AI workflow course with templates and a community to learn alongside, this is what AI Income Lab offers. The VIP plan includes the Ultimate N8N Template Library with 6,400+ workflows you can adapt. Every plan includes the Skool community of 2,900+ members, where you can ask questions while you build, and Premium and VIP include immediate access to the courses. I also answer questions and run weekly live group coaching on VIP.
